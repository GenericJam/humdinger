The creature: Mimi, a hummingbird

A comically round hummingbird with tiny wings, who dreamed of being an opera singer. Named after the warm-up “mi-mi-mi”. Its feathers take the colour of the note you’re singing; its beak opens with loudness, and on big notes it closes its eyes for the aria. Pupils look up when you’re sharp, down when you’re flat. The crest is the Oplings’ curl.
